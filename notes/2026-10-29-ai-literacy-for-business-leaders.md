## Organizational readiness
- Using AI ≠ activity. Using AI and being ready to scale AI are not the same thing.
- Pilots don't prove readiness:
  - Data gaps: data unsuitable for AI
  - Context dependency: pilot succeeds in one unit, fails in another
  - Hidden differences: underlying data, processes, governance and skills vary
- Each function describes readiness through its own lens:
  - Business: points to use cases
  - Data: emphasizes reporting
  - Technology: highlights infra
  - Governance: references policies

Main question: does the organisation have the capabilities to execute its AI strategy, consistently and at scale?

Readiness is multidimensional: Strategy, Data, Governance, Infra, Talent, Culture.

Data is the most misunderstood dimension. Data fed to dashboards might not be AI ready.

What is AI-ready data?
- Reliable: consistent, accurate
- Governed: clear ownership
- Secure: protected and compliant
- Accessible: available to the right systems
- Fit for purpose: appropriate for the specific use case

## Responsible AI: 4 core ideas
1. Lifecycle practice: not a one-time review
2. Guiding principles: guided by a small set of widely accepted principles
3. Identifiable bias: no tech expertise required
4. Governance: defines roles and responsibilities

### Principle 1: Lifecycle practice
- Propose: define goals and risks
- Collect data: gather representative data
- Develop: train with governance
- Deploy: release with safeguards
- Monitor: track performance and bias

### Principle 2: 5 core values
- Fairness: must not create unjustified disadvantages for particular groups
- Transparency: understand how the system is built, the data it uses and its decisions
- Accountability: a team owns the outcomes
- Privacy and security: protect the information that powers the system and the people affected by it

### Principle 3: Where can bias arise
1. Training data
2. Model design
3. Human interpretation

### Principle 4: Governance
- Define standards
- Review high-risk cases
- Assign ownership
- Require evidence
- Create escalation paths

# Lesson: Build your AI leadership strategy
Date: 2026-10-29
Source: handwritten notebook (transcribed)

## Portfolio aligned to the North Star
What separates an AI bet from an experiment.
1. North Star Principle: strategy starts from business, not from AI
2. Bet vs Experiment: difference between a real AI bet and a shallow one
3. Three Distinct Objects: strategy, framework and roadmap, and why they're confused
4. Where value lives: the real work of realizing AI value

## North Star
- Business core strategy -> treat AI as fuel for it -> aligned to KPIs that already measure success
- Start from a real goal or bottleneck, then ask whether AI helps
- Genuine bet: tied to a value driver (revenue, margin, risk or CLTV)

## Three objects
- Strategy: the why and where. Which goals AI serves and which bets matter most
- Framework: how to decide the method of choosing and testing bets
- Roadmap: the when. The chosen work sequenced over time
- Go deep and narrow: betting strategy
- Separate the objects: strategy, framework and roadmap are distinct
- Most AI value lives in people, process and culture, NOT MODELS

## Strategy steps
1. Pull the business objectives that have been committed (revenue, cost, cycle time)
2. For each: primary KPI; a guardrail metric (which cannot get worse when we chase the KPI); accountable executive sponsor
3. Map each objective to the workflow or decision point where the metric is won or lost (not the owning dept; the place where the result changes)
4. Assess AI candidates: which business metric will improve and by how much?
5. Concentrate: 2 or 3 high priority bets
6. A bet is tied to a North Star KPI; tool-driven ideas are experiments
7. Set the baseline and gate before building

## The Translation Method
Pull goals -> Attach KPIs -> Map workflow -> Ask AI -> Pick 2-3 bets -> Park the experiments -> Set a baseline and Go/No-Go
- Which goal does this serve? Every metric should connect to an objective
- What KPI would move? If no metric shifts, the initiative lacks strategic grounding
- Strategy or standalone experiment? Distinguish aligned bets from disconnected pilots

This translates to: where AI matters most, when to invest, how you'll know it works.
